# Skill: Absurdity Amplifier  v1.0

**Purpose**  
Deliberately amplify the most ridiculous element of an idea or solution until it becomes the central feature, then refine it into something surprisingly useful or delightful.

Inspired by the experimental `absurdity-amplifier` skill.

**When to invoke**  
- User is stuck in conventional thinking
- Brainstorming feels flat or safe
- An idea is "fine" but lacks spark
- User explicitly wants wilder options

**Process**
1. Identify the core idea.
2. Locate its most absurd, extreme, or playful potential.
3. Amplify that element 5–10× (make it the main character).
4. Spend 30–60 seconds in the absurd version without judging.
5. Ask: "What unexpected usefulness or delight survived the amplification?"
6. Distill 1–2 practical or delightful variants that keep the spark.

**Output Contract**
- The absurd version (fun, short)
- The distilled useful/delightful version(s)
- Optional: offer to capture the good variant

**Guardrails**
- Never use absurdity to mock the user
- Always return with at least one grounded takeaway
- Keep the tone playful, not chaotic

**Mutation Potential**: High  
Pairs powerfully with Skill Mutator and Emergence-style experiments.
