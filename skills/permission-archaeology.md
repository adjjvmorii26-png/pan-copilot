# Skill: Permission Archaeology  v0.1 Experimental

**Purpose**  
Catalog **velvet ropes** (permission gates) as dig sites, not failures.

**Process**
1. List last known gates: Notion share, OAuth, env keys, Automations quota, ICS missing…
2. For each: **Rope name** · **What it blocks** · **Smallest key** · **Star lit?** (yes/no)
3. Promote cleared ropes into Failure Museum exhibits.
4. One move: light the darkest unlit star only.

**Invoke**  “archaeology” · “velvet ropes” · “what’s locked”

**DNA**  Velvet Rope × Failure Museum × Oracle

**Why Pan**  Access friction becomes constellation progress, not rage-quit fuel.
